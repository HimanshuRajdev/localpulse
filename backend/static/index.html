Which businesses is a neighborhood missing?

Pick a spot and LocalPulse looks at every business around it. Then it checks which kinds are rarer here than in the surrounding area.

  1. Businesses come live from OpenStreetMap and get sorted into 14 categories.
  2. Each category's share is compared with its share in the surrounding 4 to 6 km (a location quotient), so a downtown is judged against its own city.
  3. Census population shows how many people each business serves, and 1.8M Yelp reviews show what customers complain about in each category.
  4. An LLM rules out gaps with obvious explanations, then suggests three businesses for the rest.

Coverage depends on how well OpenStreetMap is mapped locally, so treat the scores as a starting point.
